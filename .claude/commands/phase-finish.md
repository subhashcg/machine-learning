---
description: Close a phase — verify every step merged and the project defended
argument-hint: "[phase number, e.g. 2]"
---

Load the `tutor` skill, then close phase $ARGUMENTS.

1. Verify every step in the phase is ticked in `CURRICULUM.md` and merged into
   `main`. List anything outstanding and stop if there is any.
2. Verify the phase project exists, runs from a clean checkout, and has its
   written defence — decisions made, and where the result fails.
3. Review that writeup as a skeptic, not a cheerleader. Name the weakest claim
   in it.
4. Ask the learner to explain, without notes, the one idea from this phase they
   would most need in the next. Check the answer.
5. Summarise what the phase actually produced: artefacts, and capabilities they
   did not have before.

Do not start the next phase.
